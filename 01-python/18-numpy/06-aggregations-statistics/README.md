# Day 51 - NumPy Aggregations & Statistics

Welcome to Day 51.

Today we focus on summarizing data with NumPy. The goal is to reduce arrays into useful statistics that describe their center, spread, and extremes.

## Learning objectives

- Understand aggregation as a way to summarize many values with fewer values.
- Use `np.sum()`, `np.mean()`, `np.median()`, `np.min()`, `np.max()`, `np.std()`, and `np.var()`.
- Use `np.argmin()` and `np.argmax()` to identify positions of extreme values.
- Interpret `axis=0` and `axis=1` correctly.
- Apply feature-wise and observation-wise statistics to tabular data.
- Recognize why statistics matter in data science and machine learning.
