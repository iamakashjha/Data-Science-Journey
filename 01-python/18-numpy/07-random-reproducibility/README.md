# Day 52 - NumPy Random Numbers & Reproducibility

Welcome to Day 52 of your Data Science Journey.

Today you will learn how random numbers are generated, how to control them, how to sample from data, and why reproducibility is essential in data science and machine learning.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the role of randomness in data science and ML.
- Use `np.random.random()`, `np.random.rand()`, `np.random.randint()`, `np.random.uniform()`, and `np.random.normal()`.
- Generate arrays and matrices with random values.
- Use `np.random.choice()` for sampling.
- Understand seeds and reproducibility.
- Learn the modern NumPy generator API: `np.random.default_rng()`.
- Apply random generation to realistic synthetic datasets.
