Topic 1: Percentiles

A percentile tells you:
What percentage of observations are below a value?

Example:
90th percentile

means:
90% of values are below you
10% are above you


**Real Examples**
1. Exam Scores  
If you're in the: 95th percentile  
You scored better than 95% of students.

2. Salary  
If your salary is: 80th percentile  
You earn more than 80% of people in that group.


**Topic 2: Quartiles**

Quartiles divide data into four equal parts.
Q1 = 25th percentile
Q2 = 50th percentile (Median)
Q3 = 75th percentile

Visualize:
|----Q1----|----Q2----|----Q3----|
0%        25%        50%        75%       100%


**Topic 3: Interquartile Range (IQR)**

IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of data.

Formula:  

IQR=Q3−Q1

Why is this useful?  
Because it's much less affected by extreme outliers than variance.
