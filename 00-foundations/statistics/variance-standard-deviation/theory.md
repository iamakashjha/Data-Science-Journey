Step 1: Understand the Problem

Consider:

Class A
70, 70, 70, 70, 70

Mean = 70

Class B
20, 50, 70, 90, 120

Mean = 70

Both have the same mean.

But are they similar?

No.

Class A is very consistent.

Class B is highly spread out.
