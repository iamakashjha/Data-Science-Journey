# Reflection Questions
### 1. What is a vector?

A vector is an ordered collection of numbers that represents a quantity with both magnitude (size) and direction.

**Example:**

A car moving 60 km/h toward the north can be represented as a vector.

### 2. Why are vectors more than lists?

A vector may look like a list of numbers, but those numbers have mathematical meaning.

**For example:**
```
[3, 4]
```
is not just two numbers. It can represent:

- A position on a map
- A movement in space
- A person's features in AI
- A force acting on an object

Vectors can be added, scaled, compared, and used to measure similarity.

### 3. What does magnitude represent?

The magnitude of a vector represents its size, strength, distance, or intensity.

**Examples:**
- Speed of a vehicle
- Strength of a force
- Distance traveled
- Confidence of a prediction

For vector [3, 4]:

Magnitude = √(3² + 4²) = 5

### 4. Why are vectors important in AI?

AI systems convert real-world data into vectors because computers understand numbers.

Vectors are used to represent:
- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Users
- Products


This allows AI models to:
- Find similarities
- Make recommendations
- Perform searches
- Classify data
- Generate content

For example, modern AI models convert words and sentences into high-dimensional vectors called embeddings.

### 5. Give five real-world examples of vectors.
**Example 1: Navigation**

A GPS tells you:
```
Move 5 km North-East
```
This movement is a vector.

**Example 2: Wind**

Weather reports may describe wind as:
```
20 km/h toward the West
```
Both speed and direction are included.

**Example 3: Force**

When pushing a box:
```
50 Newtons toward the right
```
The force is represented as a vector.

**Example 4: Aircraft Movement**

An airplane traveling:
```
800 km/h toward Delhi
```
Velocity is a vector.

**Example 5: AI Embeddings**

A sentence like:

"I love machine learning"

may be represented as:
```
[0.21, -0.45, 1.32, ...]
```
This vector captures the meaning of the sentence and helps AI compare it with other sentences.
