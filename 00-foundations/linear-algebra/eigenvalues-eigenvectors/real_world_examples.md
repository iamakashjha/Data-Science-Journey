
## Step 6 — Real Applications
**1. PCA**

Reduce:
```
100 features
```
to:
```
10 important features
```
while preserving most of the information.


**2. Image Compression**

A photo may contain millions of pixel values.

Eigenvectors help keep the important patterns while reducing storage.

**3. Face Recognition**

Different images of the same person share important patterns.

Eigenvectors help identify those dominant patterns.

**4. Search & Recommendations**

Many dimensionality reduction techniques are built on eigenvalue concepts.
