1. What is uncertainty?
- Probability is a measure of uncertainty.
- Probability helps quantify uncertainty.
2. What is a sample space?
- The set of all possible outcomes.
3. What is an event?
- An event is a subset of outcomes.
4. Why do we define sample spaces first?
- Defines the boundaries: It establishes the total set of all possible outcomes.
- Provides the denominator: You cannot calculate a probability without knowing the total number of possibilities you are dividing by.
5. Why do repeated experiments approach theoretical probability?
- Law of Large Numbers: A mathematical law stating that larger sample sizes yield results closer to the true average.

- Averages out noise: Short-term streaks and random fluctuations cancel each other out over thousands of trials.
